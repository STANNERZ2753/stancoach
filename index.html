Bus Company — Simulator (Grid Map)

Manual steering (WASD / arrow keys) plus on-screen touch controls for phones. Routes have stops — drive the bus to pick up passengers and earn fares. Autosave enabled.

Map & Driving

Route editor: click on the map to place stops. When finished, click Start Drive and steer the bus through stops to collect passengers.
